    How PDF Bench works

    1. Add PDFs. Drop them anywhere on the page, or use + Add PDFs. Installed as an app, you can also open PDFs with it from your file manager.
    2. Tick the files you want to work on. Shift-click a tickbox to tick a whole range. Drag ⋮⋮ to reorder.
    3. Pick a tool (Split, Merge, Combine, Rename). The preview tells you exactly what will happen before you press the button.
    4. Download. Results are added to the list and ticked, so “Download ticked as ZIP” grabs just what you made. You can also chain tools — split, then merge or rename the pieces.

    Everything runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and after the first visit the app works offline.
